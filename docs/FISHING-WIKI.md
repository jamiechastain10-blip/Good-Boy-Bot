## Quick start

1. Type **`/fish`** (or press **Fishing** on the `/arcade` panel). Your fishing menu opens. Only you can see it.
2. Press **🎣 Cast**. A form asks where to fish and which rod, bait and tackle to use. It remembers your last setup.
3. Something bites: play the [reeling mini-game](#reeling-the-mini-game) with the buttons.
4. Caught it? It goes in your **🎒 Bag**. Sell fish for coins, then buy better gear in the **🛒 Shop**.
5. Fill your **📖 Collection** with all 30 fish.

Your menu has four buttons: **Cast**, **Bag**, **Shop** and **Collection**.

## Reeling (the mini-game)

```
[░░░░■■■■◈■░░░░░░░░░░]
Catch   ▰▰▰▰▰▱▱▱▱▱  52%
Tension ▰▰▱▱▱▱▱▱▱▱  20%
```

- `■` is your **catch zone**. `◆` is the **fish**. It turns into `◈` when it's inside your zone.
- The fish moves every time you press a button, and now and then it **bolts** a long way.
- **Catch** starts at 30%. Get it to **100%** to land the fish. If it drops to **0%**, the fish gets away.
- **Tension** goes up when you reel. If it fills up, **the line snaps** and the fish is gone.

| Button | What it does |
|---|---|
| ◀️ **Pull** / ▶️ **Pull** | Move your zone left or right. If the fish is inside afterwards, you gain a little catch **and tension drops**. If not, you lose a little catch. |
| 🎣 **Reel** | Fish inside the zone: **big catch gain**, some tension. Fish outside: you **lose catch** and tension shoots up. |
| ✂️ | Cut the line and let the fish go. |

**Other ways to lose a fish**
- Leaving it alone for **45 seconds** lets it swim away.
- After **80 presses**, the fish tires you out and escapes.

**How to win:** follow the fish with Pull, and only Reel when `◈` is showing. If tension is getting high, Pull a couple of times while the fish is in your zone to calm the line down, then Reel again.

## Rarity

Rarer fish fight harder: they move more, pull harder on the line and take more catch away when you miss. They also give much more XP.

| Rarity | Base chance | XP | How hard it fights |
|---|---|---|---|
| ⚪ Common | 55% | 10 | Easy |
| 🟢 Uncommon | 25% | 20 | Easy |
| 🔵 Rare | 12% | 45 | Medium |
| 🟣 Epic | 5.5% | 100 | Medium |
| 🟡 Legendary | 2% | 250 | Hard. *"Something **huge** is fighting back!"* |
| 🔴 Mythical | 0.5% | 600 | Very hard. *"The water is glowing... this is no ordinary fish."* |

**Luck** from your rod and bait makes rarer fish more likely. Here's how the chances change:

| Setup | Total luck | Common | Uncommon | Rare | Epic | Legendary | Mythical |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Driftwood Rod, no bait | 0% | 55% | 25% | 12% | 5.5% | 2% | 0.5% |
| Driftwood + Glowgrub *or* Carbon Striker + Glass Shrimp | 35% | 41% | 25% | 16% | 10% | 5% | 1.7% |
| Rimeglass Rod + Glowgrub | 70% | 30% | 23% | 19% | 15% | 9% | 3.9% |
| Cinderspine Rod + Glowgrub | 85% | 26% | 22% | 19% | 17% | 11% | 5.1% |
| Voidreach Rod + Glowgrub | 115% | 19% | 19% | 20% | 19% | 15% | 8.1% |

## Giants, Mutated and Corrupted

| Bonus | Chance | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| 📏 **Giant** | 2% | 1.6× heavier than normal, so worth more. +25% XP. |
| 🧬 **Mutated** | 2% | 1.3× heavier and **2.5× the value**. +50% XP. |
| 🕳️ **Corrupted** | 0.4% | **5× the value**. +50% XP. |

- A fish can be Giant **and** Mutated or Corrupted at the same time.
- **Corrupted** chance goes up by +3% in **The Hollow Deep** and +3% with the **Void Lure**. Use both for about 6.4%, or about 12.8% with the Four-Leaf Charm.
- The **Four-Leaf Charm** doubles the chance of both Mutated and Corrupted.

Legendary, Mythical, Mutated and Corrupted catches post a short line in the channel so everyone can see. It never pings anyone.

## Selling your catch

Open **🎒 Bag** to see your 10 most valuable fish and what everything is worth.

- **Sell all but rare finds** sells everything **except** Legendary, Mythical, Mutated and Corrupted fish, so you don't sell a trophy by accident.
- **Sell everything** sells the whole bag.

Fish you sell **stay in your Collection**. Selling never removes a fish from your log.

## Collection and leaderboards

**📖 Collection** shows all 30 fish, place by place. Fish you've caught show their name and your **heaviest** one. Fish you haven't found yet only show their rarity.

## Tips

- **Starting out:** fish Lantern Bay with Earthworms and save up for the **Reedspring Rod** at level 3. The wider zone makes everything easier.
- **Only Reel on `◈`.** Reeling while the fish is outside your zone costs catch and builds tension fast.
- **Calm the line.** Pull while the fish is in your zone to lower tension before it snaps.
- **Watch for bolts.** Fish sometimes jump far. Get your zone back on them before reeling again.
- **Glowgrub on good rods** is the best way to find Legendary and Mythical fish.
- **Steel Leader** is the best first tackle if your line keeps snapping. **Lead Sinker** is better for fish that keep escaping your zone.
- **Corruption hunting:** The Hollow Deep + Void Lure + Four-Leaf Charm gives about a **12.8%** Corrupted chance (32 times the normal 0.4%). They're worth 5× as much.
- **Don't walk away mid-fight.** After 45 seconds the fish swims off.
- **Use "Sell all but rare finds"** to keep your trophies safe.

## FAQ

**Can other people see me fishing?**
No. Your fishing menu and fights are private (since 2.3). Only rare catches post a short public line, and it never pings anyone.

**Does a restart or update lose my fish?**
No. Your catch is decided the moment a fish bites and saved after every button press. If the bot restarts mid-fight, the fight ends, but your bag, gear, level and collection are always safe.

**Do I lose my bait if the fish gets away?**
Yes. Bait is used when you cast, whether you catch something or not.

**Can someone else press my buttons?**
No. Anyone else who presses them gets their own menu instead.

**Can I buy coins with real money?**
No. Coins are play money only. They can't be bought, sold or cashed out.

**Fishing says it's turned off.**
A server admin can turn fishing on or off in the games settings (`/modules` → games → **Fishing**).
